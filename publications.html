Journal articles

Published · 2024

Reduction of the shallow water system by an error aware POD-neural network method: Application to floodplain dynamics

M. Allabou, R. Bouclier, P.-A. Garambois and J. Monnier
Computer Methods in Applied Mechanics and Engineering, 428, 117094, 2024.

A non-intrusive reduced model for 2D flood simulation: POD extracts a few dominant flow patterns, and neural networks learn how they evolve, without opening the simulation code. By accounting for the projection error, the method stays accurate with few modes and small networks, even for convection-dominated flows. Applied to the flooding of the Aude river (southern France), it reproduces water levels and discharges of the full 2D model and opens the way to real-time simulation of large floodplains.

Submitted manuscripts

Submitted · 2026

Registering wetting fronts by dilation: a non-intrusive shallow-water reduced-order model with global mass closure for flood forecasting

M. Allabou
Submitted to Advances in Computational Mathematics.

Moving flood fronts are hard to capture with a fixed reduced basis. This work maps the moving wetted area onto a fixed reference domain before compression, and recovers the front position from the conservation of the total water volume. The flow is then described with three to four times fewer modes, and water-depth errors are 12 to 20 times lower than with standard POD–NN. An ensemble of 10,000 flood events runs in about three minutes on a laptop, making probabilistic flood forecasting affordable.

Manuscripts in preparation

In preparation · 2026

Physics-guided regime-adaptive reduced-order modelling for shallow-water flows and flood uncertainty quantification

M. Allabou

Instead of a single global model, this framework uses several local reduced models, each adapted to a flow regime (wetting fronts, hydraulic jumps, overbank flow), and a physics-guided classifier that selects the right one during the simulation. On a 2D flood benchmark with 400 scenarios, it halves the error of a global ROM, and it shows that reliable ROMs for nonlinear flows depend as much on choosing the right local model as on the model itself.

PhD thesis

Defended · March 2026

Reduced-order modeling of the two-dimensional shallow-water equations using an error-aware POD–neural-network method: application to flood dynamics

M. Allabou
PhD in Applied Mathematics, INSA Toulouse – Institut de Mathématiques de Toulouse, 2026.

A non-intrusive reduced model for 2D shallow-water flows, built from POD and neural networks, with a key ingredient: learning the projection error, which keeps the model accurate with few modes and compact networks. The thesis also reduces the input signals (upstream hydrographs) with BiLSTM autoencoders, forming a complete reduction chain from inputs to outputs. Tested on two real 2018 floods, the Aude river (France) and the city of Abidjan (Côte d’Ivoire), the surrogate predicts floods on large meshes in a few seconds, and points to overflow-dominated urban flows as the next challenge.
